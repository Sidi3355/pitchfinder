# Design critic, iteration 1, 11 Sep 2026

Basis: the iter1 screenshot set (20:41) and the build served until 20:58 (index-CxNpjjh-.js). dist was rebuilt at 20:59 (index-D58ndWDV.js) while this review was running; where that newer build differs, the line says so. Mobile numbers are CSS px on a 390 px iPhone 13 viewport; desktop is 1280 px.

P0: The page is wider than the phone on every screen (all screens, mobile). The header auth group ends at x=483 on a 390 px viewport, so the document is 483 px wide, the page pans sideways, "Sign in" is cut off at the screen edge (x=332 to 401) and "Sign up" (x=409 to 483) is entirely off-screen. Every fixed layer inherits that 484 px layout width, which is what clips the drawer and the dialog below. Newest build drops Sign up and still overflows by 11 px (Sign in ends at x=401).
P0: The pitch drawer is clipped on the right by 94 px (pitch-drawer, mobile). The drawer is 420 px wide at x=64; the ENCLOSURE, FORMATS and CHANGING ROOMS values, the Save button (x=346 to 413) and, with a group set, the journey minutes ("about 26 min" ending at x=464) are all off-screen. The Close button sits at x=434 to 464, wholly invisible, so the only exits are Escape or browser back. The group journey time is the product's core number and it cannot be read on the device the group will use. Newest build: drawer is full width and Close is visible; it still runs 11 px past the screen.
P1: The sign-in dialog does not fit and its own close control is unreachable (opened from any card's heart, mobile). It is 400 px wide at x=42 to 442, the ✕ at x=388 to 418 shows 2 px, and the page pans sideways when the Username field autofocuses. Escape does not close it, and after three Tabs (Password, Sign in, "New here?") focus leaves the dialog for the header behind the scrim. Tapping the scrim is the only way out, and nothing says so. Newest build: 361 px wide, Escape works, copy is now passwordless.
P1: Only the title text opens a pitch (home, both). Each article.card is 358 x 121 px with a border and 8 px radius, but its sole hit target is the 14 px title link, 18.9 px tall; tapping the card body does nothing (URL unchanged, no drawer). The card promises a tap it does not honour, and on mobile the target is a fifth of the card's height.
P1: The heart is invisible and does something unexpected (home, both). "Save pitch" is 15 x 17 px in #d1d5db on white, 1.47:1, the lowest contrast on the site; when signed out it launches the sign-in dialog with no hint on the card. The only save affordance cannot be seen and then punishes the tap.
P1: Rank badges on the first three cards only, so the list has two left edges (home, both). Titles sit at x=59 for ranks 1 to 3 and x=31 from rank 4 down; the scan line breaks at the fourth item and the fourth card looks like a different component.
P1: Results are indistinguishable (home-group, both). For a two-person group the top ten held three identical pairs: "Cage pitch in Canary Wharf" at 2 and 3, "Cage pitch in Rotherhithe" twice, "Cage pitch in Southwark" twice, each pair with identical meta, facts and fit; the screenshot set shows the Canary Wharf pair at 1 and 2, and the unfiltered list has "Cage pitch in Southwark" at 4 and 5. A user choosing between them has nothing to choose on.
P1: Every card says its facts twice (home, both). "Free · floodlit · fit 90" is followed by a green line "free to play · floodlit · bookable online"; with a group the journey appears as "up to 30 min away (est.)" and again as "29 min average journey" in a different unit. Cards are 121 px (149 px with a group) for two lines of information, and on mobile 1.9 of them fit under the map.
P1: The default ranking has no location anchor (home, both). With no group set the heading is "Top-rated pitches" over 3,124 results and "Show more (3,114 remaining)" pages through all of London ten at a time; number one is a cage in Holloway whether the user is in Croydon or Barnet. The first screen ranks something nobody asked for.
P1: The budget slider at rest reads "BUDGET PER PERSON £0" (filters-tab, both). £0 means no cap here (results stay at 3,124) but reads as free only, and the value is written into every shared URL as budget=0. An ambiguous control in its default state.
P1: The error state exposes the exception and offers no action (home, both). "Couldn’t load pitch data (Failed to fetch). Refresh to try again." sits in a red box with no retry control while the map, tabs and controls stay live as if nothing happened.
P1: Em dashes in six places (about and group-tab, both): five in the About body copy and one in the empty group state ("No players yet ... until you add some"). Breaks the house copy rule on the two pages that carry the most prose.
P1: Focus is browser default or fainter (all screens, both). Links and buttons rely on outline:auto (1 px dark on desktop, amber on Android emulation, screenshot after three Tabs lands on "About"); text inputs get a 2 px ring in rgba(21,128,61,0.35), roughly 1.6:1 against white, weaker than the default it replaces. Scheduled for iteration 9; worse than expected only on the inputs.
P1: Touch targets under 44 px, 38 of 39 controls on the mobile home screen (all screens, mobile). Measured: header links 35 px tall; Sign in and Sign up 29 px; Results, Your group, Filters tabs 39 px; card title links 18.9 px; hearts 15 x 17; map Zoom in, Zoom out, Find my location 29 x 29; Show more 36 px; Player name and Home area inputs 36 px; Walk, Cycle, Public transport, Drive 29 px; Add player 36 px; travel-mode selects 129 x 31; Remove × 30 x 30; checkboxes 15 x 15 in 20 px rows; both sliders 16 px tall; Reset 29 px; Back to results 85 x 15; Open in OpenStreetMap 148 x 26; Book at venue, Plan a game, Save 41 px; Share 36 px; drawer Close 30 x 30. Scheduled for iteration 9; recorded as the baseline.
P2: Eight type sizes on one screen (home, both): 11, 12, 12.5, 13, 13.5, 14, 16 and 17 px, with reasons at 12, meta at 12.5, facts at 13, tabs at 13.5 and titles at 14 all within 2 px of one another. Hierarchy is carried by colour and weight rather than size, and nothing sits on a scale.
P2: Secondary text sits on the AA floor at very small sizes (all screens, both). #6b7280 on white is 4.83:1 at 12.5 to 13 px (card meta, "3,124 match", filter helper lines, "PITCH TYPE" labels at 12 px); 11 px #6b7280 on #f7f8f7 is 4.54:1 (map attribution, fact labels "PRICE", "operator's published rate"). Passing by 0.04 at 11 px on a phone is not legible in daylight.
P2: The loading state is a text box that displaces the tabs (home, both). "Loading pitch data…" in a 46 px bordered box above the tabs pushes them to y=393 on mobile and y=114 on desktop; when data lands they jump up 57 px and the box vanishes. No skeleton for the list, and the map area shows nothing at all.
P2: The empty result gives no way out (no-results, both). "No pitches match the current filters. Try widening them." has no action; Reset lives on another tab and is always enabled, even with nothing set, so it never tells the user whether there is anything to reset.
P2: Drawer actions wrap to an orphan (pitch-drawer, both). Book at venue 130 px, Plan a game 116 px and Save 67 px fill the 379 px row and Share drops alone onto a second line; on the desktop pitch page the same four buttons sit on one row, so the two views of one pitch disagree.
P2: The attribution pill covers the map (home, mobile). A 331 x 25 px white pill at y=302 sits over the bottom 9% of a 279 px map and over the markers there; with so little map, every row of it matters.
P2: Header count and list count disagree (home, desktop). The header says "3,148 pitches across London", the unfiltered list says "3,124 match"; 24 pitches are missing and nothing says why.
P2: "Back to results" reads as a caption (pitch-page, both): 13 px #4b5563, no underline, 85 x 15 px, indistinguishable from the "Shoreditch · Powerleague" line two rows below it.
P2: The pitch title repeats its own subtitle (pitch-page-osm, both): "Cage pitch in Ladbroke Grove" over "Ladbroke Grove". Generated names for unnamed OSM pitches add a line without adding information.
P2: The About page is a hero (about, both): 32 px headline "Every football pitch in London, on one map.", a primary "Open the map" button above the fold and a grid of type cards; the bar says no hero sections and no marketing copy. On mobile the headline wraps to two lines at 32 px.
P2: Signed-out profile is marketing tone (profile-signed-out, both): "Your games, in one place" centred at 22 px over Sign up and Sign in. Newest build replaces it with "YOUR GAMES / Sign in to save pitches, keep your group and organise games" and one Sign in button, which is closer to the bar.
P2: The group form sits above the group (group-tab, both). Name, area, mode and Add player take about 190 px at the top, so after adding two people the list of who is coming sits below an empty form; in the mode control "Public transport" is 125 px wide and Walk, Cycle and Drive are 53 to 58 px, so the segments look unequal by design rather than by content.
P2: "Best for your group of 2" is paired with "3,124 match" (home-group, both). The count belongs to the filters, the heading to the group; side by side they read as 3,124 best pitches.
P2: The fit score does not separate results (home-group, both): the top four group results score 66, 66, 66 and 65, and the top five unfiltered results 90, 78, 74, 74, 74. Scheduled for removal in iteration 6; noted because it currently adds a number to every card without ordering anything.
P2: The map takes 42% of the mobile viewport (home, mobile): 279 px of 664 px, tabs at y=336, list heading at y=392, first card at y=425. Bottom sheet is scheduled for iteration 3; recorded as the baseline it must beat.

Verdict: On desktop the structure is calm and sound, but on mobile the header overflow breaks every screen and hides the group journey time, so for the phone user this iteration is different rather than better.
