# Product critic, iteration 1, 11 Sep 2026

Scope: judged on the iteration 1 build (assets/index-BwNvxZXD.js, the build behind agent/screenshots/iter1) on the iPhone 13 profile and at 1280px. dist/ was rebuilt with iteration 2 work at 20:59 while this walk was running, so a re-test against the current dist may differ.

- P0: The default budget of £0 per person silently removes every venue with a known price: the landing shows "3,124 match" while the desktop header says "3,148 pitches across London", ticking "Commercial centre" returns "2 match" (Commercial pitch in Romford and Goals Dagenham, both "Price on booking"), and Powerleague Shoreditch at £78/hour only appears once "BUDGET PER PERSON £0" is dragged up (/?t=commercial gives 2, /?t=commercial&budget=15 gives 17). A group looking for a bookable five-a-side pitch on a Thursday night sees an app that appears to have almost no bookable venues and never learns why.
- P0: The top bar needs 484px on a 390px iPhone: "Sign in" is clipped and "Sign up" is off-screen (mobile-home.png), every page scrolls sideways by 93px, and in the iPhone 13 profile the layout stretches to 484px so the pitch drawer opens from x=64 to x=484 with its right quarter cut off: the journey times read "al", the Save button and the Changing rooms box are sliced (mobile-pitch-drawer.png). The one screen that answers "how far is it for each of us" cannot be read on the phone it is built for.
- P0: A shared pitch page for an OpenStreetMap pitch has no address, street, postcode or directions link: /p/osm-w797977594 says "Cage pitch in Whitechapel", "Whitechapel", then "Open in OpenStreetMap" (mobile-pitch-page-osm.png shows the same for Ladbroke Grove). 2,949 of the 3,148 pitches are unnamed like this, so the link you would paste into WhatsApp cannot tell five people which cage it is or how to get there.
- P1: Share drops the group: tapping Share on the drawer hands over http://host/p/osm-w797977594 with no ?g= parameter, while the "Open this pitch as a page" link keeps it, so a friend opening the shared link gets no "Journey times" section at all and none of the reasons the pitch was picked.
- P1: The card body does not open the pitch: only the 19px-high title link does, on mobile and desktop (the card's cursor stays "auto"). On a phone most taps land on the "Free · up to 26 min away (est.)" line and nothing happens.
- P1: Nothing on the first screen says what to do: the landing is a map of empty rings, three tabs "Results | Your group | Filters" and a list headed "Top-rated pitches" ranked by "fit 90" with no group set (mobile-home.png). There are no ratings anywhere in the product, so "Top-rated" is untrue, and a first-time user has no reason to tap "Your group".
- P1: The ranked list is not a short list: with Sam (Peckham) and Ali (Hackney) the top ten are ten unnamed cages, three "Cage pitch in Whitechapel", two "Cage pitch in Canary Wharf" with identical lines ("Free · up to 30 min away (est.) · floodlit · fit 66"), two "Cage pitch in Rotherhithe", two "Cage pitch in Southwark", then "Show more (3,114 remaining)". Collapsing duplicates is iteration 4, but it blocks the loop now: nobody can say "in" to "Cage pitch in Canary Wharf" when there are two.
- P1: The number one pick for that group, "Cage pitch in Whitechapel", shows "SURFACE Not known" and "FLOODLIGHTS Not known" in the drawer yet outranks floodlit astro cages at the same journey time, and the card hides both unknowns ("Cage · Whitechapel"). For a September evening kick-off, unknown floodlights is a reason to drop a pitch, not to put it first.
- P1: The card reasons are the same on every card and partly redundant: "up to 26 min away (est.)" and "26 min average journey · similar journey for everyone" sit together on the first card, and "similar journey for everyone" appears on nine of the top ten. Reasons that do not differ between results do not help anyone choose between them.
- P1: "fit 66" and "fit 90" are unexplained numbers on every card and nothing in the app says what they mean (removal is iteration 6). It is the one figure a sceptical friend will ask about, and there is no answer.
- P1: The group list never shows where each player is from: after adding Sam from Peckham the row reads "Sam · Public transport · ✕" (mobile-group-tab.png) and the URL stores only coordinates (?g=Sam~~51.474~-0.069~t), so nobody can check that "Hackney" resolved to the right place or spot a wrong area once the link is shared.
- P1: After "Add player" the new row lands at the bottom edge of the panel (top at 640px on a 664px screen, Sam's row cut in half in mobile-group-tab.png) while the form stays put, emptied; the only feedback is the "1" badge on the tab. It feels as if the tap did nothing.
- P1: Filtering is blind: the Filters tab shows no result count and has no "Show N pitches" action, so finding a free floodlit pitch is Filters, "Floodlit (evening games)", "Free to play only", then back to Results (4 taps) before you learn it is "100 match". "Bookable online only" returns "2 match" because of the £0 default, which reads as a bug.
- P1: The empty state is a dead end: /?t=commercial&free=1 shows "No pitches match the current filters. Try widening them." with no button, and "Reset" lives only on the Filters tab (mobile-no-results.png).
- P1: Save in the drawer opens a "Sign in" modal asking for "Username" and "Password" that then admits "Profiles are currently stored in this browser only. Cloud accounts with sync are on the roadmap." Asking for a password to write to this browser is misleading, and roadmap copy does not belong in a modal; accounts are iteration 2 but the wall is live now.
- P1: "Book at venue" and the "bookable online" chip on the two named pitches at the top of the landing list (Market Road Football Cages, Hackney Marshes) open generic council pages (islington.gov.uk/sports-parks-and-trees/sports-and-physical-activity, hackney.gov.uk/hackney-marshes), not a booking flow. The list's best-looking results promise a booking they cannot deliver.
- P1: Journey times are straight-line guesses shown to the minute ("about 26 min", "up to 33 min away (est.)", "29 min average journey"), and the caveat "Estimated from straight-line distance and typical speeds. Check a journey planner before you set off." is small grey text at the bottom of the drawer. The "(est.)" label was this iteration's fix; a precise minute count still reads as a fact people will plan around.
- P2: It takes 8 taps plus four typed fields (two names, two areas) to reach a ranked list for two players on the phone; pressing Enter in the area field adds the player and is the quickest route, but nothing says so.
- P2: Every copied URL carries "&budget=0" although nobody touched the budget, and a three-player link is 90 characters of tildes and semicolons; fine in the address bar, ugly in a chat.
- P2: The pitch page title and OG title repeat themselves ("Cage pitch in Southwark: Cage / MUGA in Southwark") and use one generic image per type (/og/cage.png), so every shared cage looks identical in a WhatsApp preview.
- P2: Postcodes are rejected with the same red line as nonsense: "SE15 4AB" and "Narnia" both get the red line "Pick an area from the suggestions" (geocoding is iteration 5; the message could at least say postcodes are not supported yet, and it ends in an em dash, which the house style bans).
- P2: Tap targets: the ♡ "Save pitch" button is 15 by 17px, the filter checkboxes 15 by 15px, the drawer's ✕ 30 by 30px; all below the 44px people can hit with a thumb.
- P2: "Show more (3,114 remaining)" pages a 3,000-item list ten at a time; nobody reads past ten, so the remaining count is noise.
- P2: The map takes 42% of the phone screen (279 of 664px) to show empty cluster rings, leaving room for 1.7 result cards above the fold (bottom sheet is iteration 3).
- P2: /p/does-not-exist keeps the generic title "PitchFinder: where to play football in London" and says "That pitch is not in the current dataset."; "dataset" is developer language on a page a friend reaches from a stale link.
- P2: Desktop shows "3,148 pitches across London" in the header and "3,124 match" in the sidebar on the same screen with no filters set (desktop-home.png).

Verdict: No. The shared link is the whole point of the loop, and in this version it lands the group on a nameless "Cage pitch in Whitechapel" with no address and, because Share drops the group, no journey times, so everyone opens Google Maps anyway to work out where it is and whether it is worth the trip.
